# NGC 2403 photometry-selected zoom regions

We ranked 830 resolved entries in the existing 25 Mpc/h parent dark-matter simulation by absolute logarithmic distance from the photometric halo mass. The ranking uses the physical NFW profile from the Kroupa luminosity calibration. Changing the critical-density convention from H₀ = 70.4 to 67.36 km/s/Mpc at fixed profile gives a target mass of 1.61532 × 10¹¹ solar masses and a radius of 115.26 kpc. Both masses refer to total-matter-equivalent collisionless simulations. Critical-density conversion does not recalibrate the relation between stellar mass and halo mass for the parent cosmology. [Original mass estimate](../ngc2403_external_halo_1324385/README.md)

We traced the closest 12 entries. For each entry, we combined HOP group members and all particles inside two or three spherical-overdensity radii at redshift zero. The stored particle identities recover distinct cells on the initial 256³ parent grid. We repeated the spherical mass measurement around the densest particle and three local centroids. The selection required a bracketed density crossing at all five centers, center motions of at most 0.1 halo radii, mass changes of at most 5%, and a listed neighbor farther than three halo radii. The neighbor measure uses groups with at least 500 members and at least half the candidate HOP mass. The thresholds are operational choices for preparing pilot calculations. They do not establish dynamical equilibrium, a galaxy counterpart, or a clean evolved zoom.

Five candidates passed. The two highest mass-ranked survivors are group 434 and group 451. Their inferred masses are 1.6151 and 1.6220 × 10¹¹ solar masses. Their maximum center shifts are 0.072 and 0.053 halo radii, and the corresponding maximum fractional mass changes are 0.50% and 1.14%. Group 295 ranks second by mass alone, but its center shifts by 0.62 halo radii and its mass changes by 27.6%. The fine ordering in mass is below the physical precision of the parent catalogue.

The files `points_g434.txt` and `points_g451.txt` contain 2,196 and 2,015 parent-cell centers, respectively, in units of the periodic box side. They describe the regions traced from three halo radii including additional HOP members. They are direct inputs for the MUSIC ellipsoidal-region generator at point level 8. The parent simulation and the inferred ID-to-cell map are the same as for the historical targets 205 and 218. Grammar job 1578501 completed in 20 seconds.
