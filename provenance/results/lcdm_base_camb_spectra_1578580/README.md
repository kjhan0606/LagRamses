# ΛCDM initial spectra on the shared base grid

Grammar job 1578580 compared the saved 256³ parent and Group 205 zoom base fields with a new CAMB 1.6.5 linear calculation at z = 49. Both use Ωm = 0.315, Ωb = 0.0493017, h = 0.6736, As = 2.1 × 10⁻⁹, ns = 0.965, and massless neutrinos. The comparison uses wavevectors from 0.251 to 16.081 h/Mpc. The densities are the gas contrast and minus the divergence of the linear CDM displacement. The velocity statistic sums the power of all three components.

The realized relative gas/CDM transfer amplitudes on the parent grid agree with the corresponding input transfer ratios. In the 12.06–16.08 h/Mpc shell, the realized gas/CDM density amplitude is 0.84074329 and the input prediction is 0.84074329. The corresponding velocity amplitudes are 0.98548133 and 0.98548133.

The MUSIC input normalization is lower than the independent CAMB prediction. In that shell, the MUSIC-to-CAMB power ratio is 0.98126 for gas density and 0.96709 for gas velocity. The realized parent-to-CAMB ratios are 0.98375 and 0.96957, respectively. The realized-to-MUSIC-input ratios are 1.00254 and 1.00257. Finite-realization fluctuations affect the latter measurements. [Parent shell results](parent/shells.csv)

MUSIC's recorded z = 49 growth factor is 0.02538667 and its input z = 0 σ₈ is 0.825072. CAMB computes σ₈ = 0.02114575 at z = 49. Normalizing the z = 49 transfer shape with the MUSIC growth factor predicts a density-power ratio of 0.98118, close to the measured input ratio of 0.98126. MUSIC records a velocity factor of 396.862 km/s per Mpc/h, while CAMB's aH/h is 399.760 in the same units. Applying that factor ratio to the density prediction gives a velocity-power ratio of 0.96701, close to the measured 0.96709. MUSIC's factor also includes its scale-independent growth rate. These comparisons identify the normalization and velocity conversion as the source of the offsets within the numerical precision shown.

The existing Group 205 zoom uses the same input normalization. At 12.06–16.08 h/Mpc its base-grid gas density power is 0.98308 times CAMB, while the CDM density reconstructed from displacement is 0.92776 and the velocity power is 0.91734. The zoom's multilevel restriction modifies its base fields. The result does not establish the spectrum of the fine zoom cells. [Zoom shell results](zoom/shells.csv)

CAMB describes the density variables in synchronous gauge and the supplied velocity variables in Newtonian gauge. The velocity-power conversion uses aH/(hk) with k in h/Mpc. The two calculations use a matched parameter list, though their numerical accuracy settings and the MUSIC background approximation differ. [CAMB transfer variable definitions](https://camb.readthedocs.io/en/latest/transfer_variables.html)

Fine-level spectral validation, a RAMSES reader check, evolved particle counts, contamination, and force convergence remain open.
