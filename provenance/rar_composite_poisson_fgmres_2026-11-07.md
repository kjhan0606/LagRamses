# Native composite Poisson iteration: first compiled solves

Numerical source17ce7e3 adds restarted, right-preconditioned flexible GMRES for
the native leaf operator. It solves L phi=rhs using A=-L, a volume-weighted inner
product and a zero-mean potential gauge. Incompatible periodic RHS means are
rejected; callers explicitly remove the density mean. The initial preconditioner
is the positive two-point face diagonal, not native multigrid. Two modified
Gram-Schmidt passes reorthogonalize the Arnoldi basis. Success requires a fresh
unpreconditioned native residual, including its numerical mean, after updating
the solution; the projected Hessenberg estimate is not an acceptance test.

An opt-in solver check retains actual Newtonian deposited density by level
before the legacy QUMOND update overwrites it. From the fixed native leaf source,
it solves N, constructs the conservative phantom source from that candidate N
potential, and solves Q using the same composite operator. The nu family is
explicitly restricted to simple nu. These candidate arrays are not published to
phi/f or consumed by particles/gas/sinks. This is an elliptic solver building
block, not the final synchronous hierarchy gravity operation.

## Remaining work

Discrete recovery tests the iteration, not continuum/force accuracy. Smooth-source
resolution convergence and independent operator/force references remain required.
The test does not publish candidate potentials/forces and has not moved hierarchy
source assembly ahead of all coarse consumers. That control-flow integration,
covered-cell restriction and force construction are the next implementation
steps. Native MG/FFTW preconditioning may be needed at larger scales. MPI,
changing topology, restart persistence, synchronized evolving timesteps and
coupled hydro remain open. Legacy force outputs still use the old source path;
this numerical result is not an AMR galaxy/RAR prediction or MOND cosmological IC.
