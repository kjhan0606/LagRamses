**Q-GOAL: no.** The cold-only completion does not deliver the requested goal, and the reason is structural rather than a missing table. In kind 6 every leaf cell goes through the cold stage, and one status-50 rejection sets a chemistry failure that rolls back the level and calls `clean_stop` (`patch/lagRamses/snrt_ramses_driver.f90:1497-1518`, `:1856-1901`). So a single cell whose internal CVODE trial exceeds the molecular-cooling ceiling ends the run. A supernova dump into dense molecular gas heats it past 10^5 K and cools it back inside one hydro step, so no feedback run survives this mode. The hot atomic entry cannot absorb those cells either: it demands zero dust and zero solid charge (`snrt_chimes.f90:265`, `snrt_chimes_runtime.f90:331`), rejects any endpoint at or below 10^5 K (`snrt_chimes.f90:290`), and there is a dead band between the table ceiling and 10^5 K that neither entry accepts (`snrt_chimes_bridge.c:58` vs `snrt_chimes.f90:211`, `:257`, `:283`). Rejecting Tmol_K=1e9 was correct; the retained scan shows why (`.chimes-molecular.U6ZQCr/data-check.log` lines 8-13).

## Recommendation: B, as an explicit opt-in model. A reduces to C.

**Why A cannot be completed honestly.** The loaded H2 collisional-dissociation fit is still plausible at log T = 5 (log k0 for H2-He is -8.3, He critical density 0.09 cm^-3) but is already unphysical at log T = 6 (k0 = 5.6e-5 cm^3/s, far above the gas-kinetic limit, critical density underflowed). Lookups clamp at the grid end (`chimes_interpol.h:47-51`) and the loader clamps to log DBL_MIN (`init_chimes.c:1423`), so a "retained" network above 10^5 K silently integrates these values. The published sources behind these fits stop near 10^5 K, and nothing exists above. For the heavy molecules the situation is worse: the only thermal dissociation group in the network is H2_collis_dissoc (`update_rates.c:473-500`); CO, OH, H2O and the ions are destroyed only through generic T-dependent, ion-neutral, photo and cosmic-ray channels with no dissociation-to-atoms rate and no source above roughly 10^4 K. Molecular cooling ends at log T = 4.98 and is held constant beyond. The minimal inputs for A would be collisional dissociation rates for 20 species against H, H2, He and electrons over 10^5 to 10^9 K plus high-T line cooling. No such database exists. That is the specific blocking data, and fitting it would be invention.

**Why B is the physics anyway.** At the ceiling the dissociation-to-cooling time ratio is density independent and tiny:

| Quantity at the table ceiling | Value |
|---|---|
| Ceiling T_max | 95,499 K |
| Thermal energy, fully molecular neutral gas | 7.4 eV per H nucleus |
| Full atomization cost, x_H2 = 0.5 | 2.24 eV per H nucleus |
| Temperature after atomization at T_max | about 3.6e4 K |
| H2 dissociation time | about 6 yr × (cm^-3 / n) |
| t_diss / t_cool | about 2e-3 |

Molecules do not survive above the ceiling. A's time-dependent survival collapses to the event in B, and the only regime where instantaneous atomization fails is when the step ends before dissociation, n k Δt < 1, which is countable.

## Leanest B design and essential corrections

1. **Event rule, uniquely determined.** Atomize whenever T ≥ T_max and any of species 138-157 is nonzero (`chimes_proto.h:809-828`). Products are the neutral atoms from the existing nuclei and charge maps (`snrt_chimes_bridge.c:45`, `:124`). For molecular ions place the charge on the lowest-ionization-potential fragment, so electrons are untouched and charge is exact; do not copy upstream's electron removal (`update_rates.c:1203`), which is an unbudgeted dissociative recombination. The cost is the ATcT 0 K enthalpy-of-formation difference for that channel, one number per species, transcribed and cited, not fitted. Subtract it from thermal energy and set T' = E'/(1.5 k n') using the existing translational capacity (`snrt_chimes_runtime.f90:232-235`). No clamp is needed because at T = T_max the thermal energy always exceeds the largest possible cost. An event located earlier, for example at the start of a rejected step, is not always affordable, so the crossing must be located. No chemical-energy field is needed: the cold network already internalizes H2 formation and dissociation energy for what it processes itself (`chimes_cooling.c:351-363`, `:460`), and the event pays only for survivors.

2. **Locate the crossing by bisection on the dark substep** using the existing status-50 signal (`snrt_chimes_bridge.c:399-405`, `:675`). Each probe is one CVODE solve from the unchanged entry state, only for crossing cells, about seven probes for 1% accuracy in t*. This avoids patching upstream `chimes.c` for root finding. If the photo step alone pushes T past the ceiling (`snrt_chimes.f90:233`), the event sits at the photo/dark boundary, where T ≥ T_max holds by construction.

3. **Remainder of the step in the non-molecular network.** Run `cell_charged` with the private configuration copy's Tmol_K set to zero (`snrt_chimes_bridge.c:637-646`, `:485`), which forces the atomic branch at `chimes.c:227` even below the ceiling. Remove the hot endpoint rejection at `snrt_chimes.f90:290` so hot gas may cool through the ceiling within a step. Next step, selection by state resumes the cold network for cells at or below T_max with zero molecules, restoring formation, grain transfer and photoelectric terms. Their absence during the remainder above about 3e4 K is small and declared.

4. **Reuse the cold photo step for hot cells.** With molecular abundances zero, `chimes_band_photo_molecular_groups` reduces exactly to atomic plus grain competition and already returns grain N/E (`snrt_chimes.f90:31-41`); the dust-only step lacks those counters (`:72-80`). Pass unit shielding factors instead of calling `snrt_chimes_molecular_factors`, which rejects T above the ceiling (`snrt_chimes_bridge.c:281-282`). Drop the dust-free requirement from the hot path.

5. **Unify thresholds.** Replace the literal `tmol=1d5` in `snrt_chimes.f90:257` and the 1e5 in the private copy with `chimes_molecular_temperature_max()` so the domain is contiguous.

6. **Identity and diagnostics.** This is a material physical choice: instantaneous atomization at the ceiling, charge placement, and an atomic remainder. It must not silently change kind 6. Register a new model name through the existing selector (`snrt_spectral_contract.f90:121-124`) and bind the thermochemical values into the restart identity. Add two counters printed alongside `SNRT_CHIMES_PRIMARY_ABSORBED_EV`: atomization events, and events with n k_LTE(T_max) Δt_remaining < 10 using the loaded in-domain kLTE. No new gate, no abort.

**Verification.** Extend the existing native smoke with one heated molecular cell: nuclei and charge exact, energy conserved including the tabulated cost, endpoint T equal to the analytic value, and identical results whether the crossing occurs in the photo step, the dark step, or at entry. Then one short MPI2/OMP2 restart comparison on the irradiated fixture with a thermal dump, checking bitwise restart equality as before.
