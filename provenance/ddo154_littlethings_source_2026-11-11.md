# DDO 154 extended H I source preparation

The selected gas-rich dwarf DDO 154 lacks sufficient radial coverage in the local Leroy profile for the planned fixed-source QUMOND–Lambda-CDM comparison. The [NRAO LITTLE THINGS archive](https://bulk.cv.nrao.edu/littlethings/ddo154/HI/) lists a natural-weight H I image `DDO154_NA_X0_P_R.FITS` as well as other image and cube products. Its scientific calibration, primary-beam treatment, extent, units, geometry, relation to the adopted SPARC mass model, and appropriateness for the project remain to be established. In particular an integrated intensity map is not itself a validated surface-density or three-dimensional gas distribution.

Grammar job `1290927`, submitted from `provenance/tools/ddo154_littlethings_download.sbatch` at commit `284be87`, retrieves this public image into the unique directory `/gpfs/kjhan/RAR_QUMOND/observations/ddo154_littlethings_1290927/`, records its URL, checksum, FITS header, and basic integrity metadata. It requests 2 CPUs, 8 GiB and 20 minutes and does not run any gravity solver. Submission does not establish successful retrieval, radial coverage, calibration or a usable galaxy model. Subsequent interpretation should use the original [LITTLE THINGS data paper](https://www2.lowell.edu/users/dah/LITTLETHINGS_datapaper.pdf), the source product documentation, and an independently documented radius, distance, inclination, helium and beam convention; preserve the raw input.

Job `1290927` completed with exit `0:0` in 5 seconds; its manifest verifies and the preserved FITS file is 4,245,120 bytes with SHA-256 `7dc505fffa9e35530c7d556b6e84357e73bd7be4c17624eab82d5f4c8874b911`. The primary array shape is `(1,1,1024,1024)`, the pixel scale is 1.5 arcsec, and `BUNIT` is `JY/B*M/S`. Standard `BMAJ/BMIN/BPA` header keys are absent, but the AIPS HISTORY records `CLEAN BMAJ=3.9140e-3`, `BMIN=3.5066e-3`, `BPA=-58.11` (the conventional degree interpretation corresponds to approximately 14.09 by 12.62 arcsec and still requires source-convention confirmation). HISTORY also records PBCOR applied to `XMOM0`, then REMAG from that corrected image, with 848,052 blank pixels replaced by zero. Thus finite or zero-valued pixels are not automatically independently observed emission-free sky; a coverage/mask analysis must preserve this ambiguity and must not blindly apply a second primary-beam correction. These records support the processing history but do not certify its scientific calibration, uncertainty, or relation to the SPARC gas model.
