**Q-LEAN: over-instrumented or over-gated? Mostly lean.**
- Rejecting v3 at load unless band mode is on is justified. Outside band mode the E array is dead weight and a silent-misuse channel. Keep the existing generic failure path in the loader. Do not add a reason-code framework.
- The "stable log1p/small-interval" wording risks over-building. The per-segment endpoint weights cancel benignly. At 100,000 Angstrom with a 1 Angstrom step the weight is about 5e-6 with absolute error 1e-16, so relative error is about 2e-11. One `log1p` call is enough. Do not add a series branch.
- Out-of-band E/Q rejection is cheap because clipping to group edges bounds E/Q inside the group by construction. Use a relative tolerance, not exact comparison.
- The test list, one MPI2 run plus restart, and raw-output removal are proportionate.

**Scientific and feasibility findings**
- Interpolating Q and E separately makes the interval integral exact for the declared linear model. E/Q then varies rationally inside an interval, which is why the plan forbids interpolating E/Q. Correct.
- The shift can go negative when actual energy is below the reference. The existing nonnegative actual-energy check still guards the physical quantity, so no new invariant is needed.
- The energy integral must be clipped with exactly the same nodes as the photon integral, or the per-group E/Q bound will not hold. Assert this in the exporter test.
- Namelist growth is nine times 128 times 32 doubles, negligible.
- The report line should state that injected energy is BPASS actual while the state reference remains the contract mean, to avoid a false same-population reading.
