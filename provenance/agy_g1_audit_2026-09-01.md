**CONDITIONAL PASS**. The implementation and native contract checks were

## Findings

### Units and physical contract — OK

`stellar_native_units.f90:76-89` and
`stellar_ramses_runtime.f90:199-223` explicitly retain the current
`aexp**2` code-time convention and keep Msun, erg, and g cm/s conversions in
named routines. No supernova `1e51` normalization is hidden in the unit
boundary.

### Cumulative source and fail-closed behavior — OK

`stellar_source_increment.f90:63-80` computes
`C(age_gyr + dt_gyr) - C(age_gyr)` and rejects nonfinite or negative physical
increments. `stellar_yield_interpolation.f90:171-179` rejects queries outside
the table domain rather than clamping them to an endpoint.

### Grid and field map — OK

`stellar_yield_audit.f90:151-207` checks Cartesian corners, duplicates,
finite values, and cumulative monotonicity. The NVAR=18 map and overlap checks
in `stellar_ramses_field_map.f90:72-188` agree with the JSON contract.

### Progress transaction — structural limitation remains

`stellar_progress_contract.f90:118-135` and
`stellar_ramses_runtime.f90:178-210,391-397` update progress after the fluid
and particle writes. The repeat/restart/abort unit checks pass. A real RAMSES
checkpoint still needs to persist `indtab` consistently with the deposited
fields; this audit does not establish atomic MPI/checkpoint behavior.

### Differential coverage — required fix

`g1_native_jax_differential.py` and the result writer in
`g1_contract_test.f90` initially checked one interior point only. AGY marked
this high severity for the G1 matrix exit criterion and required exact-grid
and boundary queries.
