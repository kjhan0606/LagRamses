# Group 205 zoom target and multifluid initial-condition preparation

## Working target

Group 205 is the first feasibility candidate, not an established analogue of NGC 2403 and not an approved production halo. The level-9 one-fluid DMO zoom reached z=0 with M200c=3.0735e11 solar masses, R200c=0.14282 Mpc, 20,341 fine particles within R200c, and zero measured coarse-particle mass within 3 R200c. Group 218 remains a backup and gives a closer parent mass match. Group 205 has a more stable tested center and a more distant listed HOP neighbor. Those proxies do not establish equilibrium, morphology, assembly history, isolation, or baryonic properties. See docs/lcdm_z0_group205_group218_candidate_comparison_2026-11-06.md.

The parent candidate is not replayed exactly. The level-9 initial density in the trace region differs pointwise from the parent, with RMS difference 28.9 percent of the parent RMS. Large-scale modes correlate strongly. A common initial seed does not establish exact phase identity in a nested realization. Trace geometry, periodic coordinate shifts, matched large-scale cross-power, descendant identification and evolved contamination must be checked for every new realization.

## Source-branch and adaptive-refinement audit

The tested executable was built from `ca53ce39f455000868f0fbc25d810e45f6766a00` on `diagnostic/rar-amr-ic-refmap-fixed-20261111`, not from the current `codex/rar-qumond-amr-history` source branch. The tested commit is not an ancestor of that branch, whose source does not currently contain `ic_refmap_fixed`. Thus the reader results cannot be attributed to a binary built from the current branch. The fixed-map change also relies on the earlier `de54b156539acdfd6cfd6b8b019c7319915e89c4` diagnostic instrumentation: applying the later patch alone would refer to absent diagnostic variables, and a clean automatic application of the earlier patch fails in `rho_fine.kjhan.f90` because the branches have diverged. Integration requires a deliberate source comparison, conflict resolution, and a new build and runtime verification; neither patch has been applied to the current branch in this audit.

The present `m_refine_eff` pathway forms a refinement flag from gas and particle density relative to `mass_sph`; a single finest-particle mass threshold previously opened most of the coarse box. A production zoom needs a criterion that follows the high-resolution matter, accounts consistently for gas and collisionless mass, and prevents coarse background cells from being refined merely because their mass exceeds a finest-level threshold. The fixed IC topology preserves an initial spatial region and is a diagnostic, not such an adaptive criterion. The implementation choice and conservation, boundary, MPI and evolving-contamination behavior remain to be resolved before a long zoom evolution.

The diagnostic instrumentation and fixed-map changes from `de54b15` and `ca53ce3` were subsequently applied to the research branch using Git's three-way merge. The merged source retains the research branch's separate BIMOND density handling, while the fixed-map option remains disabled by default. Compilation and rerunning the unit-box reader with a binary from this merged branch are still required; the earlier results above use the separately recorded diagnostic binary and do not validate this integration.
