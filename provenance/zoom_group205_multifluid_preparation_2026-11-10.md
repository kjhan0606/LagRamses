# Group 205 zoom target and multifluid initial-condition preparation

## Working target

Group 205 is the first feasibility candidate, not an established analogue of NGC 2403 and not an approved production halo. The level-9 one-fluid DMO zoom reached z=0 with M200c=3.0735e11 solar masses, R200c=0.14282 Mpc, 20,341 fine particles within R200c, and zero measured coarse-particle mass within 3 R200c. Group 218 remains a backup and gives a closer parent mass match. Group 205 has a more stable tested center and a more distant listed HOP neighbor. Those proxies do not establish equilibrium, morphology, assembly history, isolation, or baryonic properties. See docs/lcdm_z0_group205_group218_candidate_comparison_2026-11-06.md.

The parent candidate is not replayed exactly. The level-9 initial density in the trace region differs pointwise from the parent, with RMS difference 28.9 percent of the parent RMS. Large-scale modes correlate strongly. A common initial seed does not establish exact phase identity in a nested realization. Trace geometry, periodic coordinate shifts, matched large-scale cross-power, descendant identification and evolved contamination must be checked for every new realization.

## Existing nested ICs and their limits

The level-11 geometry generation `1273554` covers all 4,536 trace cells in its level-8 mask. The source is `/gpfs/kjhan/RAR_QUMOND/initial_conditions/lcdm_z0_group205_r3_ellipsoid_l11_1273554/`. It is total-matter one-fluid DMO. Reader preflight `1273579` completed, but the inventory jobs `1273678` and `1273700` failed. The 1273700 log reports that `ic_del` is absent. The inventory failures remain unresolved and no level-11 evolution, evolved contamination, force convergence, hydro galaxy, or RAR result is claimed. Do not regenerate or promote the old file as a two-fluid production IC.

For the 25 Mpc/h box with h=0.6736, the previous scaling budget estimates a level-11 LCDM CDM mass of 1.991e5 solar masses and level-12 mass 2.489e4 solar masses, assuming correctly generated nested multi-fluid ICs. Mean initial gas-cell masses scale from 1.892e7 solar masses at level 8 to about 3.70e4 at level 11 and 4.62e3 at level 12. These are estimates at the cosmic mean, not fixed evolved gas-cell or star-particle masses. AMR grid refinement does not split existing DM particles. The million-particle requirement applies to a declared aperture and physical particles, with per-component counts reported. It cannot be inferred from IC mask cell counts. Even a million halo particles does not guarantee a resolved stellar disc or 0.1-0.3 kpc force accuracy.

## SMBH and galaxy physics

No SMBH is present in the DMO test or the two-fluid IC generation gate. The planned production hydro run includes SMBH seeding and growth and AGN feedback together with cooling, heating, star formation, stellar feedback and metal enrichment. Dust and ray tracing are excluded. The seed prescription, insertion epoch, mass, accretion, feedback and resolution criteria require a separately stated physical and numerical contract. Earlier paired tiny AMR tests `1279536`/`1279537`, audit `1279553`, exercised a seed BH with Bondi and thermal/jet feedback, but do not validate cosmological SMBH formation, full MPI/AMR evolution, or the future Group 205 zoom.

QUMOND remains a separate branch with the user-selected phenomenological baryon-only and Lambda-like background. A late-time QUMOND force law plus LCDM transfers does not define a self-consistent relativistic MOND cosmology. Generate model-specific baryon density and velocity transfer fields and document limitations before a matched cosmological comparison. The immediate baseline CDM two-fluid IC is a feasibility gate, not an assumption that the final galaxy and QUMOND counterpart match one-to-one.
