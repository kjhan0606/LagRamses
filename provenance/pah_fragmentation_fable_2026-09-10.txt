## Q-GOAL: minimum live capability physically supportable now

None beyond the existing H-state model. The candidate is mechanically consistent with the current semantics, but three physical inputs are missing and each alone blocks a defensible closure.

- **Mechanics already exist.** The sink/daughter path in `dust_stochastic.f90:13-37` is what H loss uses. A carbon-loss edge is one more downward axis in the same triangular solve. Fragments must be written directly into a CHIMES species carrier, the way H loss writes HI into `chemical_trial(2,:)`. CHIMES reconciliation treats molecular nuclei as locked and pushes element-ledger changes into neutral atoms, so the element ledger is the wrong route.
- **Blocker 1, fragment carrier.** With H present, which is the live state (mean H about 12 in the fixture), Murga's channel is C2H2 loss. CHIMES157 has C2 (species index 143 in Fortran) but no C2H2. Depositing C2H2 as C2 plus atoms, or into the carbon ledger, invents roughly 10 to 17 eV of binding energy per event. Only the dehydrogenated C2 channel has a consistent carrier.
- **Blocker 2, enthalpies.** Murga's E0 of 4.6 eV with deltaS of 10 cal/K/mol is an activation barrier. Neither Murga nor Lange supplies the reaction enthalpy for C2 or C2H2 loss, nor bond energies for C22 daughter H states. Montillaud's coronene bond table does not transfer. Balancing the transaction by relabelling the barrier as enthalpy is the shortcut you ruled out.
- **Blocker 3, daughter domain.** The Draine radius map in `dust_pah_radiation.f90:233-234` accepts C22 (a of 3.609e-4 micron, inside the table by 1.7 percent) and rejects C20. The tables support exactly one C2-loss generation. A hot C22 daughter loses C2 at a rate comparable to or above IR cooling near 13.6 eV, so the required rejection fires in any FUV-exposed run. Declaring C22 terminal would be the arbitrary cutoff you forbade.

Optional detail, not blockers: X-ray energy partition, anions and dications, charge-dependent barriers, super-H deltaS, fragment kinetic energy release, constant-IP approximation for C22, and the choice between Murga's Arrhenius form and the existing DOS-ratio form. That last choice must be made once and applied to both H and C channels so they compete consistently.
