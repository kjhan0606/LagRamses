**Q-GOAL.** The one physically large radioisotope term, Ni56 at 0.07 Msun per 13 to 25 Msun star against Fe56 of 0.013 to 0.024 Msun, is already moved into tracked Fe at release by the completed prompt projection. What live evolution would add is only Al26 to Mg and Fe60 to Ni. From LC18 table8 at solar metallicity:

| node | Al26 / Mg24 | Fe60 / Fe56 |
|---|---|---|
| v=0, 20 Msun | 0.06 % | 0.04 % |

Rotation alone changes Mg24 by a factor of three at 20 Msun. The SNRT groups stop at 10 keV, CHIMES and cooling use elemental abundances far coarser than 1 %, and dust condensation is isotope-blind and excluded here. Nothing downstream can see this change.

With the prompt-projected table selected, live Ni56/Co56 carriers would either double count Fe or force the runtime to undo the prompt projection. With the no-decay table, the runtime would re-derive the completed prompt correction with a weeks-scale delay. Either way they are redundant.
