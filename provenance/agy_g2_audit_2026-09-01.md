## Critical findings

1. No approved physical full-grid asset exists for wind, AGB, and SNII. The
   literature entries in the source matrix are candidates, not approvals.
2. The legacy H/O/Fe table and nine-row fixture cannot satisfy the 11-element,
   complete mass--metallicity--age production contract.
3. The converter correctly rejects undeclared rate histories, preventing silent
   second integration of a rate table.
4. SNIa and PISN remain independent disabled/unsupported event paths; neither
   may fall through to ordinary IMF integration.
5. The 11-element model must be explicitly published as reduced chemistry, or
   the omitted species must be added and their impact quantified.

## Required external input to close G2

- Select the final physical source family and version for each required
  channel, including wind, AGB, and CCSN mass loss/fallback/remnant details.
- Provide the actual source files and their SHA256 values, license status,
  conversion metadata, and approval identifier.
- Resolve the population convention: IMF, single-star/binary assumptions,
  lifetime mapping, channel boundaries, and the reduced-chemistry scope.
- Convert the approved sources to the canonical 32-field full Cartesian grid,
  including age-zero and explicit upper-age coverage, then pass the Python and
  native audits.
