## Findings and verdict

**Point 1, condensation and SN shocks: already correct, only gates need relaxing.** The step order is dust mass, then RT/CHIMES, then drag, at `patch/lagRamses/amr_step.jaehyun.f90:978`. The dust mass advance reconciles the new gas inventory into CHIMES with the transported ion state preserved, at `patch/lagRamses/dust_mass_runtime.f90:354`, and zeroes the shock and fresh transients once at line 397. Fresh ejecta are staged by the stellar runtime. Kind7 already uses the CHIMES heat capacity for the grain temperature at line 169. Indispensable fixes are the gate at `snrt_chimes_runtime.f90:119`, the spectral-mode forcing in `mkrun.py:799` that sets shocks false and condensation zero, and the generator message at `patch/cuRamses/aux/ramses_nml_generator.py:840`. Make them transition-conditional, following the growth pattern already at lines 120 to 122 of the initializer.

**Point 2, sublimation: already wired, only gates need relaxing.** The split vacuum step, the shared latent reference across growth, sputtering and shocks, and the coupled IR closure all exist. The driver reconciles released atoms into the trial chemistry before commit at `snrt_ramses_driver.f90:2174`, writes bins, species and total at commit, and the next step rebuilds opacity from the committed bins. Gas energy is conserved through the exchange term, so heat capacity needs no extra handling. The mutual exclusion of coupled sublimation with relative motion is already enforced in the live stage validator and in the namelist reader. Indispensable fixes are only the gates at `snrt_chimes_runtime.f90:119`, `snrt_ramses_driver.f90:336`, `mkrun.py:669`, and the generator. State explicitly in provenance that grain inventory is frozen during the photo step and sublimation acts afterwards in the same step. That is a first-order split, not coupled opacity.

**Point 3, relative grains: two real defects, both indispensable.**

- **The cold stage discards staged energy.** The molecular stage reads the cell row from the persistent state and computes single-fluid kinetic energy at `snrt_chimes_runtime.f90:402` and 412. With relative motion, kinetic work already staged into the trial thermal energy at `snrt_ramses_driver.f90:1444` would be lost. Fix: accept a staged row and use the phase-aware kinetic energy exactly as the charged stage does at lines 446 to 473 of the same module.
- **Momentum is never applied in kind7.** The impulse block at `snrt_ramses_driver.f90:1372` runs before the cold stage and reads transport moments, which are zero in kind7 because absorption is handed to CVODE at `patch/lagRamses/snrt_runtime_backend.f90:537`. The cold stage writes grain energy afterwards at line 1527 with no impulse. The per-phase counters in the photo operator are therefore required, not optional. The existing block at lines 1397 to 1415 is the correct template for debiting kinetic work once and passing the remaining heat to the common receiver.
- **Scattering would be duplicated.** Kind7 transport scatters in-kernel with stationary grains and no work. With moving grains, zero that scattering the same way absorption is zeroed, then call the existing work-conserving moving scatter after the cold stage. The cold stage returns group-summed rays, so use group-level moving scatter with node-weighted per-phase optical depth. Record that as an explicit closure.
- The IR side needs nothing. The live stage already skips isotropic scatter when phase data are present and adds phase work to the gas at line 2129.

**Point 4, admission and checkpoint.** Relax only the checks named above plus the sink admission at `patch/lagRamses/read_hydro_params.f90:402`, allowing condensation with sinks and nothing else. Update `mkrun.py:669` and the generator at lines 840 and 875 together. Restart identity already binds the transition bank hash and the sublimation identity, and the new counters are trial-only, so no checkpoint format change is needed.

**Verdict: approve bundle 1 with the fixes above.** Indispensable: the staged-row cold stage, the per-phase counters with a post-cold-stage impulse block, the scattering swap, keeping shocks and relative motion excluded with sinks, and the gate relaxations at the listed lines. Optional science extensions, not completion conditions: node-resolved moving scatter, per-phase diagnostics, and extending the sink material map.
