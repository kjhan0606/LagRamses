# P6 dust grain thermal balance and one-pass IR candidate

Status: conditional candidate; static JAX/P5 only.  This is not an
astrophysical dust-mixture approval and does not feed IR photons back into
transport or live RAMSES.

`snrt_dust_thermal_v1` is a provenance-validated sidecar.  It contains a
strictly increasing temperature grid, emitted power per reference-mixture H
nucleus, temperature-dependent energy fractions for configured IR groups,
temperature-dependent emission-weighted photon energies, and an explicit
out-of-band energy fraction.  The tracked fractions plus the out-of-band
fraction must sum to one.  The thermal sidecar is bound to the active DUST-1
opacity sidecar by exact group edges, source-table SHA-256, and dust mass per H.

The Draine candidate is generated by
[`tools/build_draine_dust_thermal.py`](tools/build_draine_dust_thermal.py).
The derivation is explicitly `4*pi*C_abs(E)*B_E(T)` under a single equilibrium
temperature assumption.  It is not a separate emissivity measurement and does
not include stochastic PAH/small-grain heating.

The static operator in [`snrt_core/dust.py`](snrt_core/dust.py) adds the CMB
background power from the same table, solves the monotone local balance with a
fixed 32-step log-temperature bisection, and returns an out-of-range mask for
the runner.  It is called inside each existing P5 thermochemical subcycle.
New IR energy is split into tracked and out-of-band ledgers and photon rates
use the thermal table's own mean photon energies.  Zero local absorption emits
no new IR and returns a 0 K sentinel.  Out-of-range cells fail in the runner;
the jitted operator does not silently clamp them.

Enable the candidate with:

```text
--dust-opacity-metadata <provenance-pinned snrt_dust_opacity_v3>
--dust-scattering isotropic
--dust-thermal-metadata <snrt_dust_thermal_v1>
```

The output records `thermal/dust_grain_temperature_k`, tracked and untracked IR
energy, `sources/dust_ir_photon_rate_cm3_s`, and the integrated photon/power
ledgers.  It also records maximum current IR optical depth and the fraction of
dust-bearing cells above unity as honesty diagnostics for the one-pass
assumption.  The current scalar uses the source-weighted opacity group and is
therefore a conservative upper bound for the emission-weighted IR opacity.
The IR source has attribute
`dust_ir_transport_semantics=recorded_not_transport_reemitted`.

The CMB background is evaluated from the same power curve at
`T_CMB=2.7255/scale_factor`.  For this candidate, net power
`P(T)-P(T_CMB)` is distributed using the fractions at `T`; the exact
differential group split would subtract the group-by-group CMB emission
fractions.  Therefore tracked IR is conservatively under-reported near the
CMB, and this approximation must be replaced or explicitly accepted before
the IR source is transported.  The current 5 K lower table boundary also
means this path requires `T_CMB >= 5 K` (approximately `z >= 0.83`).

The thermal balance is not added to gas thermal energy, H/He chemistry, or the
DUST-1 absorption/scattering ledgers.  Recursive IR transport, dust-gas
exchange, stochastic heating, source obscuration, native Fortran/live coupling,
and production AMR/MPI/restart qualification remain later promotion gates.
